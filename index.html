Meet the trainer behind the treats

Hi, I'm Chloe, and I believe in positive reinforcement training. I am passionate about helping dogs and their owners build lasting, trust based relationships.

My training methods are force-free, reward-based, and adapted to suit your dog's personality. Whether you're working on puppy foundations, lead skills, recall, reactivity, confidence building or muzzle training, I'll guide you through it at a pace that suits you both.

My dog, Zuri, is the reason behind everything I do. She is reactive through fear to certain things, and this inspired me to start training. Through our journey, we built a deeper connection, and I found my passion for helping other dogs and owners do the same.

I offer 1-2-1 dog training and hope to one day expand my business to include a secure dog field and an indoor training space, providing a safe and enriching experience for every dog.

I am a registered dog trainer with the Animal Behaviour & Training Council (ABTC), with over six years of hands-on experience working with and training dogs through two of the UK's largest animal welfare charities.

My Mission

Why positive reinforcement works

Reward-based training is a kind, and respectful way of training. When dogs learn that good things come from training, they want to join in, try harder and repeat the behaviours in the future. There's no force involved, just trust that builds session by session.

Builds confidence

Dogs learn to think and work things out for themselves, they will trial behaviours or tasks to see if they get a reward, helping boost their emotions and make them feel good.

Strengthens your bond

Training becomes something you both look forward to, and builds trust between you and your dog.

Safe for every dog

Gentle methods matter most for nervous, anxious or reactive dogs, so every session moves at your dog's pace.

I love what I do

I've learnt so much over the years and believe training should be positive, practicle and fun. My passion comes from watching dogs thrive and families grow closer through trust, patience and consistency.
