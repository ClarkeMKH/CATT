Why fit matters

A well-fitting muzzle changes everything

We understand that a well-fitting muzzle can be a great tool for safety, confidence and clear communication between you and your dog. That's why we provide muzzle fitting sessions for The Muzzle Movement — to help you find the ideal style, size and fit for your dog.

Safety

A correctly fitted muzzle keeps your dog, other dogs and people safe — while still allowing your dog to pant, drink and take treats comfortably.

Confidence

When the fit is right and the experience is positive, your dog can relax — and so can you. Muzzles open up the world, they don't shrink it.

Clear communication

A visible, well-fitted muzzle tells others your dog needs space — clear, kind communication that helps every walk go more smoothly.

During your session, we'll try different styles and sizes, and you'll leave knowing your dog is comfortable, protected and ready to thrive.

How it works

Your fitting journey in three steps

From first measurement to a muzzle at your door — a simple, low-stress process built around your dog's pace.

1
First · Measure

Measure at home

It's really helpful if you can take a rough measurement of your dog's nose before our session. You can use the guide from The Muzzle Movement team to see how it's done. Having a stranger measure your dog can sometimes feel overwhelming for them, and we want to minimise stress wherever possible to keep the experience positive. If measuring at home feels tricky, or you'd prefer some in-person support, just get in touch and we'll happily arrange a time to help you.
